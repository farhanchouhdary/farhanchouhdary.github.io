Three ways onto the trail

Service 01

Guided Treks

Multi-day treks across Deosai, Nangma Valley and K2 Base Camp, each led by a certified guide who has personally walked the route in three separate seasons. Pace, camps and rest days are set by the terrain — not a fixed brochure schedule.

PKR 28,000 / person, 3-day Deosai route
  • Certified guide & support crew
  • All meals on trail, camp-cooked
  • Tents and sleeping mats provided
  • Satellite communicator carried by guide
  • Permit & park entry fees

Jeep Safaris

4x4 routes into Shimshal and Khaplu with an experienced mountain driver, reaching valleys that most travel companies never take clients to. Ideal for travellers short on time or trekking with family.

PKR 18,500 / vehicle, 2-day Shimshal route
  • 4x4 vehicle, seats up to 5
  • Experienced mountain driver-guide
  • Fuel & road toll costs
