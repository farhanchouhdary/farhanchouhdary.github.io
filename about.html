About Trail & Compass

Three friends, one shepherd trail, six years of routes

How a mapping hobby around Skardu turned into Gilgit-Baltistan's guide-led trekking outfit.

Our History

Founded in Skardu, 2019

Trail & Compass began when three childhood friends — Imran, Zubair and Fatima — got tired of watching trekking groups pass through Skardu on itineraries written by agencies who'd never actually walked the routes. They started by re-surveying the shepherd trail up to the Deosai plateau, marking water sources and safe camp spots by hand.

By 2021, word had spread enough that they hired their first full-time guide crew. Today the team includes eleven guides, four cooks and a rotating pool of porters from villages across Skardu, Shigar and Khaplu.

2019

First route mapped

The Deosai shepherd trail, re-surveyed on foot over one summer.

2021

First full-time crew

Hired permanent guides and cooks; retired the "friends helping friends" era.

2023

K2 Base Camp added

Two years of permit work and acclimatisation testing before the first group departed.

2026

412 trekkers guided

Still capping every group at 8, on purpose.

Our Mission

Put good routes and fair wages in the same hands

We exist to run treks the way the people who live in these valleys would run them — paced for the terrain, honest about risk, and priced so that guiding pays better than any alternative work in the region. A route isn't "done" until it's been walked in three different seasons.

What We Stand On

Four values, non-negotiable

Pace Over Speed

We build in acclimatisation days even when it costs us a booking.

Local First

Guides, cooks and porters are hired from the valleys we trek, always.

Honest Grading

If a trek is genuinely hard, we say so before you pay a deposit.

Tested, Not Trending

Every route is walked in three seasons before it's ever sold.
